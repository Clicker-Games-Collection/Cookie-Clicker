Dive into the charming world of **Cookie Clicker**, an addictive incremental game where your mission is to generate an endless supply of cookies!

## How to Play

Your journey starts with a giant cookie at the center of the screen. Click on this cookie to accumulate delicious treats and watch your total cookie count soar! Each click allows you to make strategic decisions that enhance your cookie production.

## Key Features

- **Upgrades**: Invest cookies in powerful upgrades that boost your production rate and increase the value of each click. Explore the Upgrades section for immediate benefits!
- **Buildings**: Automate cookie generation by purchasing buildings. Each structure contributes to your cookie output over time, allowing you to gather cookies even while you’re away.
- **Golden Cookies**: Look out for golden cookies that appear randomly! Clicking these grants temporary bonuses, such as increased production and enhanced clicking power.
- **Achievements**: Complete various achievements to unlock valuable upgrades, including milk upgrades that improve your overall production rate. Track your progress easily!
- **Prestige System**: As you progress, consider using the prestige option to reset your game for prestige points, unlocking powerful Heavenly Upgrades that offer lasting benefits.

## Strategy and Progression

Experiment with different combinations of upgrades and buildings to maximize your cookie output. **Cookie Clicker** is designed as an idle game, meaning it continues generating cookies even when you’re not actively playing. Leave the game running in the background and return to see your cookie empire grow!
